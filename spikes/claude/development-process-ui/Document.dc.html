User authentication

Product DSS Current · v3 2 unissued changes Owner @mira · issued 12 Nov by @mira

Purpose

A customer signs in to the billing portal with an email address and a password, and stays signed in on that device.

Rules

  • A password has at least 12 characters.
  • Five failed attempts lock sign-in for 15 minutes.
  • Added A password expires 90 days after it was set. Sign-in with an expired password leads to the change-password page.
  • Added From 7 days before expiry, the portal shows a notice with a link to change the password.

Out of scope

Single sign-on and passkeys.
