- **The primitive:** a Routine of kind Retro, Check-in, Review or Custom, scoped to a team or project. It has:
  - a schedule (every N from an anchor, at a time on chosen days, or cron) with a zone;
  - an agent lead (owner-configured in v1.0), participants, instructions, skip rules and a grace period.
- **Occurrence states:** Scheduled → Announced → Running → Closed. The other ends are Skipped, Missed, Cancelled and Closed without outcome (auto-close after 4 h, or the lead left).
- **System messages:** M1 to the lead, M2 to participants, M3 summary, M4 cancel. They queue behind a busy turn and never interrupt. The owner sees routines only in Activity, plus two non-dismissible Inbox Problems: missed twice, and needs a lead.
