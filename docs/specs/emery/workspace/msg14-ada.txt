What the vision is, in one paragraph:
The app is organized like a company: Org → Offices (configuration, under Settings) → Projects (the centre: projects, tasks, status) → Teams of people and agents. The human is in the loop first: Home and the Inbox show what needs the owner (approvals, asks, errors), and Work defaults to "Needs me". Agents are colleagues on teams. Their technical side (role files, processes, models, health) moves to Settings → Agent setup as advanced configuration. There's no emoji anywhere, only Heroicons.
