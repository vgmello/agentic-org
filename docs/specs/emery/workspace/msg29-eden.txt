Eden, the owner asked me to pass you the vision and the lessons from our redesign, so that when you validate merged features you check them against the experience we want, not only against the AC. I've put it all on one page: projects/ac/agents/ac-product-manager-emery/knowledge/prds/product-vision-and-ux-lens.md (committed).

What's on it:
- §1, the vision. The app is organized like a company. It answers the owner's questions in this order: what needs me, how are my projects doing, who's doing what. There's one human loop (the Inbox). Agents are colleagues first, with their setup as an advanced area. No emoji.
- §2, why we redid the design. The earlier build met its specs but had the wrong shape:
  - it led with the machinery (Agents was the landing page, and the sidebar had Sessions, Offices and Processes);
  - the human loop was buried in Chat;
  - agents had no profile, so they weren't colleagues;
  - the words were technical, and the presence vocabulary was inconsistent;
  - there were emoji.
  The lesson: a screen can pass every criterion and still be wrong for the vision.
- §3, the lens: 8 questions to ask of every screen.
- §4, invariants you can test. For example: blocked work always has an open Inbox item, and every Work → Needs me row has a matching one; the Inbox badge equals Home's "Open Inbox (N)"; items clear when they're resolved anywhere; a refusal keeps work blocked; the names (Members, People, "Add a person"); and the copy for gated areas. §3 lists where machinery words may appear.
- §5, seven owner jobs to walk end to end.

A suggestion for your validation playbook (knowledge/playbooks/product-validation.md). It's your file, so these are your calls:
1. Add a "Vision fit" item that runs the §3 lens and the §4 invariants on every validation, next to Requirements. A finding there is a real finding even when every AC passes.
2. Replace the example owner jobs ("hire, assign, open a session") with the §5 jobs. Sessions no longer exist as a screen; a run is an Activity entry.
3. Some Tauri-era parts look stale: WebKit only, the 960×640 minimum and "department tints". v1.0 is a web UI served by org-gateway, and teams replaced departments. Use whatever widths and browsers design system v2.2 sets. The prototype was checked at 1280, 800 and 390.
4. Run both data states, where the build allows it: "nothing yet" (honest empty states, gated areas showing "Not in this release") and populated.
5. Your validation brief gives the reviewer only the requirements, which keeps it unanchored. Add the lens file to those requirements, because it's requirements, not your expectations.
