## 2. Users and jobs

- **The owner, coming back:** "What needs me? What broke? What shipped? Are we on pace?" In two minutes, with a link to every claim.
- **The owner, any day:** "What did today look like?"
- **The owner, with people in the org:** "What time is it for them?"
- **An agent:** nothing new to do. No agent writes the digest.

## 3. Goals, non-goals and success

**Goals:**
- On return after an away of at least the owner's threshold, Home answers "what happened" in **one card**, and every line links to its source.
- Problems are reported as problems, captured when they happen, even if they resolved before the owner returned.
- Every time in the UI is in the viewer's time zone (schedule times keep the schedule's own zone, Z2), and the owner can see any person's local time.

**Non-goals:**
- per-agent leaderboards, rankings or scores;
- email, push or any notification outside the app (mobile push is proposal L3);
- tracking the owner beyond app activity: no OS idle, camera, location or calendar;
- invitations or sign-in for people (a separate future PRD; see §7);
- an agent-written summary;
- a personal time zone for agents (an agent's profile shows its schedule's zone, Z1).

**Success**, measured 30 days after release **[A]** (needs a before-count from chat history):
- The owner's "what happened / status?" messages to agents in the first 30 min after a return fall by half.
- On at least 80% of qualifying returns, the card is marked as seen or one of its links is opened within 15 min.
- Zero digest lines without a source link, and zero reads of away/back times by anyone but that account (tests, §5).

## 4. The experience

### 4.1 Time zones

**[O] Decision Z1. Every account and every person has an IANA time zone.**
- **An account's (the owner's)** zone is detected from the browser at the first sign-in with no zone saved (this covers accounts from before TZ-1) and saved. It's editable in the account menu → **Your preferences → Time zone** (D21: there's no member profile for the owner, so "Settings → Profile" becomes this). The control is a searchable list showing "São Paulo · UTC−3 · 14:05 now".
- **A person's** zone is set by the owner on the person's profile (People → person → Edit), next to name and contact. People never sign in (SIA:293-295), so nothing is detected; empty shows "Time zone not set".
- **Agents** have no separate personal zone. An agent's profile shows its schedule's time zone and the current time there ("Schedule time zone: São Paulo · 14:05 now"); the schedule keeps its own zone (ORG:274-280). An agent without a schedule shows no time zone. Offices have no zone today, so office settings show none; if offices gain one later, it shows there.

**[O] Decision Z2. Times show in the viewer's profile zone, not the browser's.** One display rule:
- **Event lists outside the card** (feeds, Activity entries): relative within 24 h ("2 h ago", "in 40 min"). Older: absolute, 24-hour as the UI already does (`messaging/model.ts:479`): "Mon 9 Nov, 18:40".
- **Headers, ranges, local times, logs, and every time inside a card or problem line:** always absolute ("09:12 – 12:52", "Unblocked 11:02", "Local time 14:05", a run log's timestamps). **Card times:** a time inside the card (header ends, problem and Delivered lines) shows the time alone only when it falls on the viewing day. Otherwise it reads "Ddd D Mon, HH:MM": "Unblocked Tue 10 Nov, 11:02" viewed on Thursday 12 Nov, "Unblocked 11:02" viewed the same day. Relative times are only for event lists outside the card.
- **Schedule times** (an agent's next scheduled run, and a routine's slots and deadlines, including skipped and missed slots; `ceremonies-and-retros-v1.md` §4 has the full list) show in the schedule's own zone ([F] ORG:274-280), always with the full date, the zone's name as the time zone list shows it (its city, e.g. "Lisbon"; "UTC" for UTC) and the offset in force at that instant: "Thu 12 Nov, 18:00 Lisbon (UTC+0)". A schedule time never uses "Today", "tomorrow" or a relative time, and never drops the date, even on the viewing day, because the viewer's day and the schedule's day can differ. Schedule times inside the Home card follow this rule too, not the card-times rule. Two things are not schedule times: a recurrence description carries no date ("Daily at 18:00, Lisbon time"), and the current-time clock on an agent's profile (Z1) is a local time.
- The full date, time and offset are in the tooltip.
- A person's profile and hover card show "Local time 14:05, UTC−3" (once TZ-4 ships, v1.1); the owner card on the Teams index and Org chart (D17) shows the owner's.
- **DST:** times are stored as instants; the offset shown is the one in force at that instant. Example: New York on 2026-03-08, clocks jump from 02:00 to 03:00. An event before the jump shows "01:30 UTC−5"; one after shows "03:30 UTC−4". Durations are real elapsed time, so 01:30 to 03:30 that night is "1 h 0 min".

**[O] Decision Z3. Travel and two devices: the profile zone wins, and the UI offers to switch.**
- When the browser's zone differs from the profile's, a neutral banner (not amber) reads: "Your computer is on Lisbon time (UTC+0). Show times in Lisbon time? **Switch** · **Keep São Paulo**".
- Keep is remembered on that device for that browser zone, so it doesn't nag; a new browser zone asks again.
- Two devices in different zones: both show the profile zone; each may offer the switch; nothing changes until the owner chooses.
- A switch changes how times display everywhere and where future days start (§4.4). Each day starts where the previous one ended, so days never overlap or leave a gap; the day of a switch ends at the next midnight in the new zone, so it can be shorter or longer than 24 h. Past daily summaries keep the zone they were built in, labelled with it.

### 4.2 Away and back

**[O] Decision Z4. Active and Away.**
- **Active:** the app is open and visible in any tab on any device, with input (pointer, key, scroll, touch) in the last 10 minutes. Activity means input in any tab on any device.
- **The away window starts at the last input** on any device. Away is **confirmed 10 minutes later**, focused or not: a visible but unfocused window counts as no input. Away is confirmed at once when no tab on any device is visible. Being Active on any device ends Away. The return is the first input on a visible tab after Away is confirmed; input made before the tab was hidden doesn't count.
- **The header time is the last input** ("09:12 – 12:52 · 3 h 40 min"). The duration and the digest threshold are measured from it, and events after the last input are in the digest.
- **HQ unreachable:** applies to a tab that was already open. Its activity while it can't reach HQ still counts. The app reports it when it reconnects, so no false away window is created. This is a requirement; Rowan owns the mechanism (§5.7). If HQ is down at load, the web app itself is unavailable ([F] SIA:259). A tab closed while HQ is unreachable loses its activity report. The false window is accepted, and the owner can mark it seen.
- **Forced re-sign-in:** a session expiry (`docs/design/gateway-api-v1.md:93`) neither starts nor ends an away by itself. Input on the sign-in screen is the return, clamped as below.
- **Clocks:** the **server clock** is authoritative for live input. Reports sent on reconnect or at sign-in carry client-observed times, which the server clamps to its own disconnect and reconnect times. Rowan owns the mechanism (§5.7).
- Away and back are per **account**. Several accounts can own one organization (SIA:297-305); each signed-in account has its own away/back times, its own digest and its own "seen" state. How accounts map to owner members is Rowan's call (§7 Q1).

**[O] Decision Z5. The digest threshold is a preference.** Account menu → Your preferences → "Show what happened while I was away after: **10 min · 30 min · 1 h** (default) **· 4 h · Off**". An away of at least the threshold counts. With Off, no card appears, and Activity → While you were away lists windows of at least 1 hour. Turning the threshold to Off keeps any unseen card until it's marked seen, and that card still merges later qualifying aways (at least 1 hour). A threshold change applies to future windows only.

**[O] Decision Z6. Privacy.** Away and back times, the digests and the daily summaries are private per account: each is visible only to that account. They aren't in the org event log, the org Activity feed (the digest page under Activity is separate), the directory, exports, agents' context or replication to satellites.
**[O] Decision Z10. What agents can see.**
- Agents keep today's coarse presence (`online`/`offline`, DIR:35 (Online) and DIR:47 (Offline)) and `last_seen_at` ([F] DIR:141). Nothing changes there.
- Said openly: coarse presence can be watched over time, so an agent that polls it can infer roughly when the owner is in the app. This PRD accepts that and adds nothing to it.
- Agents can read each owner account's **time zone and local time** on the owner's directory card, and a person's on theirs, so they can time messages (no 3 a.m. pings).
- Away/back windows, their durations and the digest are never exposed to agents.

### 4.3 The "While you were away" card

**[O] Decision Z7. Placement.** On return after an away of at least the threshold, Home leads with the card, **above Needs you's content, never replacing it**. Needs you stays directly below, unchanged. Amends D1 and AC-IA04-1.

**Header, one format everywhere:** "While you were away · 09:12 – 12:52 · 3 h 40 min". The first time is the last input, the second is the return, and the duration runs between them. No relative words ("yesterday", "today") appear in any header. Each end follows the card-times rule (Z2): a time on the viewing day shows alone, any other gets its date. Viewed on the return's day: "While you were away · 09:12 – 12:52 · 3 h 40 min", or "While you were away · Mon 9 Nov, 18:40 – 12:52 · 66 h 12 min" when the window began on an earlier day. Still unseen on a later day (viewed Friday 13 Nov), both ends are earlier than the viewing day, so they get dates; when both ends fall on the same day, the date prints once: "While you were away · Thu 12 Nov, 09:12 – 12:52 · 3 h 40 min".

**Elapsed spans, one format.** Every elapsed span in the card and its Activity pages (the window length, a merged card's span, "blocked 1 h 40 min") reads exactly "{h} h {m} min", with minutes only under 1 h ("40 min"). The one exception is Speed's median cycle time (AW-9, AC-AW9-1), an aggregate metric, which uses `project-insights-v1.md`'s shared duration formatter (§4.2).

**First line, only when items wait on the owner now:** "3 things need you", in amber, linking to the Inbox. N is the number of open, actionable Inbox items; neutral notices, such as a budget reset, don't count. It's the only amber in the card and the only Needs-you element in it; the card has no Needs you section, because Home's Needs you card sits right below. This line appears only on the Home card, never on a historical window opened from Activity.

**Problem kinds.** Each kind starts and resolves on a defined event.
- **Resolve rule, all kinds on a task or bug:** a problem also resolves when the item is Done (bug: Fixed) or Cancelled (bug: Closed), with neutral text: "Done {time}" or "Cancelled {time}" for a task; "Fixed {time}" or "Closed {time}" for a bug, never "Cancelled {time}". The kind's own exits are in the table.
- **Blocked** is the one kind where any move out of Blocked resolves it. Reassigning a Blocked task moves it to To do and clears the wait (tasks-v1 §4.4), so it resolves as "Reassigned {time}".
- **Resolved-text rule:** a resolved problem shows the kind's text with an absolute time in the viewer's zone, by the card-times rule (Z2); Blocked adds its duration: "Unblocked 11:02 · blocked 1 h 40 min", or "Unblocked Tue 10 Nov, 11:02 · blocked 1 h 40 min" when viewed on a later day.
- **Precedence when a problem also waits on the owner:** "waits on the owner" means any of the five cases in `tasks-v1.md` §4.1 (blocked on the owner, the owner as reviewer, an open Hand-off to the owner, an open question to the owner, or a Budget reached block or hold), or an agent paused on its daily or monthly budget (`costs-and-budgets-v1.md` req 20), as they stand at render time. So an agent's open Budget reached line gets "Needs you · see Inbox" while the agent stays paused. While the item (or agent) waits on the owner, its line is neutral. On an open problem, "Needs you · see Inbox" replaces the frozen suffix ("blocked at return"). On a resolved problem, the resolved text stays and " · Needs you · see Inbox" is appended: "Unblocked 11:02 · blocked 1 h 40 min · Needs you · see Inbox". The "N things need you" line stays the only amber. Once the owner handles it, the line falls back to its frozen text (red if still open) plus any live badge.
- **Populations:** Blocked, Changes requested, Reopened and Run error include subtasks, shown with their parent's key. "Over its usual time" is top-level only. Budget reached covers tasks, bugs and subtasks with their own budget, and agents' daily or monthly budgets (one line per agent per budget period, linking to the agent's budget on its profile: daily and monthly are separate lines, so a daily reset never hides a monthly pause); subtasks stopped by their parent's budget aren't listed separately. A block caused by a budget ("Budget reached" or "Parent budget reached") is reported only as Budget reached, never also as Blocked. New Critical or Major bug covers bugs only. **Repeats in one window, every kind:** one line per item (or per agent per budget period, for an agent's daily or monthly budget) per kind per window. The line shows the latest occurrence's state (open, or its resolved text), and when there was more than one occurrence it adds "· N times" after the kind text, before any "Needs you · see Inbox": a run that failed at 10:00 and 10:30 and recovered at 11:00 reads "Recovered 11:00 · 2 times". On an open problem the count sits after the kind text and before the frozen suffix (or the "Needs you · see Inbox" that replaces it), in that order.
- **Fix task reopened:** a fix task reopened while its bug is Fixed moves the bug back to In progress (`docs/specs/work-items-ux.md`). That counts as the bug reopened from Fixed. It shows once, on the bug, with the fix task linked, and not also as the task's own Reopened. This goes beyond the badge rule in `docs/specs/work-items-ux.md`, which doesn't say the bug counts as reopened. Follow-up for Eden: show the same on the bug page (the bug reads as reopened, with the fix task linked), so the digest and the page agree.

For a run with no task: the agent's next successful run of the same trigger (the same schedule, or the same chat) | "Recovered {time}"; or "Reassigned {time}" |
| Review changes requested | the reviewer requests changes | the next Approve or Done. A resubmission to In review doesn't resolve it. A second request in the same window follows the repeats rule (Populations) | "Approved {time}" or "Done {time}" |
| Reopened | a task is reopened from Done, or a bug from Fixed or Closed · Verified. Reopening from Cancelled, or from a no-fix Closed, is not a problem | a task is Done again, a bug is Fixed again, or the item is Cancelled (bug: Closed) | "Done again {time}", "Fixed again {time}", or "Cancelled {time}" (bug: "Closed {time}") |
| Over its usual time | the item crosses the line during the window | it is Done (bug: Fixed) or Cancelled (bug: Closed) | "Done {time}" or "Cancelled {time}"; bug: "Fixed {time}" or "Closed {time}" |
| New Critical or Major bug | the bug is filed, or raised to Critical or Major, during the window. A bug already open as a problem that is raised from Major to Critical stays one problem; lowered from Critical to Major it stays open | it reaches Fixed, is Closed, or its severity drops below Major | "Fixed {time}", "Closed {time}" or "Severity lowered {time}" |
| Budget reached | a task's, bug's or subtask's own budget, or an agent's daily or monthly budget, is reached (costs-and-budgets-v1 CB-6, CB-7). A release cap is never a problem | the budget is raised, allowed once or removed; a person changes the budget's unit to a value above spent in the new unit (costs-and-budgets-v1 req 14, 23); the item drops below its limit after a cost record is moved off it (costs-and-budgets-v1 req 33); or the agent budget resets; or the item is deleted. A refused Raise or Allow once resolves nothing. For an item's budget, the general rule also applies: Done (bug: Fixed) clears it, and Stop from the Inbox cancels the task, so it resolves as Cancelled (bug: Closed) | "Budget raised {time}", "Allowed once {time}", "Budget removed {time}" (the budget is cleared), "Budget changed {time}" (a unit change above spent), "Cost moved {time}", "Budget reset {time}", "Deleted {time}" (the line stays, unlinked, because the item is gone; the one exception to "every line links to its source"); or the general text ("Done {time}", "Cancelled {time}"; bug: "Fixed {time}", "Closed {time}") |

**Bug delivery rule (this PRD owns it).** A bug's Done time is its last Fixed, the same rule as `project-insights-v1.md` §4.2 (When a bug is "Done"). A bug is delivered when its last Fixed falls in the window and, at the return, it is Fixed or Closed · Verified and hasn't been reopened since. Closed · Verified follows Fixed (`docs/specs/work-items-ux.md`), so a bug fixed and verified inside the window counts once. **Last-Done rule:** an item counts as Delivered when its last Done (bug: Fixed) in the window still stands at the return. The same holds for releases (Released) and milestones (Done). Only Done (not Cancelled) counts for a task; a Cancelled task is never Delivered. Done then reopened before the return isn't Delivered (as in project-insights AC-IN6-2). Bugs closed as Duplicate, Won't fix or Can't reproduce are not Delivered and add nothing to Progress; they show as the neutral line "Closed without a fix: N" in Delivered, counting closes in the window: a bug that is open again at the return isn't counted; otherwise each bug counts once per window.

**Historical windows.** A past window's counts, Delivered and Problems are frozen to the window (start to return, or, for a merged later window, from the previous return), except for late-synced events. **Late satellite sync:** a late event keeps the time it was stamped on the satellite and joins the window that contains that time (how satellite clock skew is handled is Rowan's call, §7 Q3). A late event stamped while the owner was active (outside any window) joins no window; it appears only in the normal Activity feed. Events stamped inside a past window that sync from a satellite after the return are added to that window's card or Activity row (counts, Delivered, Problems), each judged as of the return like any other event, and the card or row is marked "Updated later {time}" (the time of the latest such sync; same pattern as the daily summary, AC-AW7-3). The "{Office} hasn't reported since {time}" line clears once that office syncs. Late events always update the Activity row. If the Home card was already marked seen, it comes back, unseen and marked "Updated later {time}", only when the late events add at least one Problem line; otherwise it stays hidden. Each line keeps its frozen text as of the return (an open Blocked problem reads "blocked at return"). Live badges are per section and show only changes since the return: a Delivered line gets "reopened since" when the item leaves Done (bug: Fixed) after the return; a Problem line gets "resolved since" only, for a problem open at the return. A new occurrence after the return is a new problem for the next window, not a badge on this one. The Home card carries the same live badges. "Needs you · see Inbox" is live, shown while the item waits on the owner now. Progress compares the window start with the return.

- **Over its usual time** uses the definition in `project-insights-v1.md` §4.4, which owns "usual" (85th-percentile cycle time for its kind, over the last 90 days, with at least 10 done items of that kind; below the minimum, nothing is flagged). In the digest: an open top-level item whose time since it was first Started, excluding spans spent Done or Cancelled (for bugs, Fixed or Closed), crossed that threshold **during the away window**. Items already over before the window aren't repeated. When it resolves, a bug reads "Fixed {time}".
- **Every line links to its source:** the task, release, Inbox item, run log or message. The one exception is a Budget reached line resolved as "Deleted {time}": its item is gone, so the line is unlinked.
- **Narrative line** (Next): one sentence on top, such as "Kai released R-1; two tasks are blocked on Rowan", generated from the facts, each claim linked. If generation isn't available, the card shows the sections without it.
- **Mark as seen** hides the card. "Seen" syncs across the account's devices. An unseen card survives later short aways; if another qualifying away ends before the card is seen, one card covers both, so nothing is skipped. A merged card reads "{start of the first unseen window} – {return}", with the elapsed (real) time between them, not the wall-clock difference, and includes events, problems and over-its-usual-time crossings during the active gap between the two windows. Opening a link on the card doesn't mark it seen; only Mark as seen does. If the first unseen window is older than the 30-day retention, the merged card starts at the oldest kept window, and its header uses the full format with the oldest kept window's date: "While you were away · Tue 13 Oct, 18:40 – 12:52 · 714 h 12 min" (the viewer's zone is America/Sao_Paulo, which has no DST, so the elapsed time holds).
- **Afterwards:** Activity → **While you were away** lists the account's past away windows of at least the threshold in force when each window was recorded (at least 1 hour with Off), newest first (the last 30 days), each row prefixed with the return's date and showing start, return and duration ("Tue 10 Nov · 09:12 – 12:52 · 3 h 40 min"; a window that began on an earlier day: "Tue 10 Nov · Sun 8 Nov, 18:40 – 09:30 · 38 h 50 min") and opening the same sections. Each window is its own row, even when the Home card merged them; when it did, the later window's row keeps its own header, and its sections, Progress included, run from the previous window's return, so the gap is included and nothing the merged card showed is missing from Activity. The "N things need you" line shows only on the Home card, not on a window opened from Activity. The **period picker** (a relative option, or a date and time) comes Next.

**States:**
- **Empty:** "Nothing happened while you were away." The card stays on Home until Mark as seen (or until it merges into the next away card).
- **Loading:** a skeleton card in the same place, so Home doesn't jump.
- **Error:** the shared one-line error with Retry; Needs you below still loads.
- **Offline or office unreachable:** the last loaded digest marked "As of {time}", or nothing if none loaded. A satellite that hasn't synced yet adds the line "Lab office hasn't reported since 08:50; its work may be missing." The line clears once that office syncs; its events stamped inside the window are then added, and the card or row is marked "Updated later {time}" (Historical windows).

**States of the Activity pages and the time zone picker:**
- **While you were away, empty:** "No away windows in the last 30 days."
- **Daily summary, empty day:** "Nothing happened on {day}." Previous/next still work.
- **Loading:** a skeleton list (While you were away) or skeleton sections (Daily summary), in place.
- **Error:** the shared one-line error with Retry.
- **Time zone picker, search with no match:** "No time zone matches '{query}'. Try a city or a UTC offset." The saved zone doesn't change.

### 4.4 The day cycle

**[O] Decision Z8.** Every day, in the viewing account's time zone in effect at the day's start, has a **daily summary**, private to that account (Z6), with the same sections, at Activity → **Daily summary**, with previous/next day. Problems are recorded as they happen, not reconstructed at digest time, so a problem that resolved in ten minutes is still in the day. Each day starts where the previous one ended, so days never overlap or leave a gap; the day of a zone switch (Z3) ends at the next midnight in the new zone, so it can be shorter or longer than 24 h. In the daily summary, "Started while away" reads "started that day". **Next**, except the problem capture itself (AW-5), which the core card needs.

**[O] Decision Z11. Bugs and decisions.** New bugs go into Problems: only new Critical and Major bugs (the [F] severities are Critical · Major · Minor · Trivial, `docs/specs/work-items-ux.md:62`, :76 on `origin/v1`; "High" is a priority, not a severity). "New" means filed during the window, or raised to Critical or Major during it. Each is linked to its bug. Decisions join the digest as their own section when the decision log exists (proposal N5). Until then the digest has no Decisions section.

**[O] Decision Z9. Derived, not written.** Both come from existing records: work events, Inbox items and their handled times, runs, messages, releases and milestones. No agent writes or edits them.
- **The away digest** is frozen to its window (start to return, or, for a merged later window, from the previous return). Lines keep their frozen text ("blocked at return") and carry live badges only for changes since the return, per section: Delivered lines "reopened since", Problem lines "resolved since" only (for problems open at the return; a new occurrence is a new problem for the next window); "Needs you · see Inbox" is live; counts, Delivered and Problems don't change, except that events stamped inside the window that sync late from a satellite are added, and the card or row is marked "Updated later {time}" (§4.3 Historical windows).
- **A daily summary** is a snapshot, built at the end of each day in the time zone in effect at the day's start, and kept 90 days. A later zone switch doesn't rebuild it. Events that sync late from a satellite are added to their day's summary, which is marked "Updated later".

## 5. Requirements for engineering (Rowan)

1. **Per-account away windows** (start, end, server time) across devices, kept only on HQ: away data never leaves HQ, and only that account can read it (Z6). Retention 30 days for windows; daily summaries 90 days **[O]**.
2. **Problem capture at the time it happens**, including ones that clear before the owner returns (each kind's start and resolved events, as in the Problem kinds table, §4.3).
3. **A digest read** for an account and a window: sections, counts, lines and source links, computed from the sources in Z9, including satellites' events as synced.
4. **Time zone** on accounts (detected at the first sign-in with no zone saved, editable) and on people (set by the owner), validated like `schedule.time_zone` (ORG:274-280).
5. **"Usual" figures** for Speed and "over its usual time" come from `project-insights-v1.md` §4.4; this PRD doesn't define them.
6. A test proves no other account, agent or person can read away/back times or digests through any read path, by ID included.
7. **Activity while HQ is unreachable** counts: the app reports it on reconnect or at sign-in, and no false away window is created (Z4). Rowan owns the mechanism, including how the server clamps client-observed times to its own disconnect and reconnect times. A tab closed while unreachable loses its report; the false window is accepted.
8. **Time zone reads for agents:** each owner account's zone and local time on the owner's directory card, and a person's on theirs (Z10). Coarse presence and `last_seen_at` stay as today.
