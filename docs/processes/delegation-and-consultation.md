# Delegation and consultation

Proposed organization seed for EMP-7 and PROC-27. It is **draft**, so it does not
enter this repository's effective process set or change its existing review rules.
Organization provisioning copies approved seeds into its handbook with assigned doc
identities, using the existing process activation/approval flow.

1. Consider whether a bounded piece of work benefits from independent or specialized
   help. Keep work in the main session when delegation adds no value.
2. Choose deliberately: a fresh copy of your persona for an independent perspective;
   your private specialist for a recurring specialty; or a general-purpose agent for
   work that needs no particular persona. You may create a private specialist when
   useful. None of these choices creates an organization employee.
3. Give the helper a concrete outcome, scope, constraints, relevant process/standard
   references and an end condition. Share only the context it needs. An independent
   review starts without your conversation or conclusions.
4. Stay within the available time, concurrency and usage budgets. Check owned work
   at natural boundaries, cancel obsolete jobs and account for all descendants.
5. Evaluate the result yourself, resolve conflicting advice and own the final work.
   Apply the effective review/merge and autonomy processes as usual. A consult is
   advice, never an owner approval or a grant of authority.
6. When the runtime cannot provide a safe delegate, do the work yourself or escalate
   according to the effective process; an autonomy consult follows PROC-23's existing
   escalation requirement. Never bypass a runtime or spending restriction.
